PC

Program Counter

16-bit register that can count by two. On the rising edge of CLK it loads D[15:0] when /LOAD is low; otherwise it adds 2 when INC is high (bit 0 unchanged); otherwise it holds. QA drives while /OEA is low, QB while /OEB is low; outputs are independent of the clock.
